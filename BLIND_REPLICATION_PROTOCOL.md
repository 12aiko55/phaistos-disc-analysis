## Part A: The Blind Brief (send this to the specialist — nothing else)

> **Instructions for external Luwian specialist:**  
> The following structural statistics describe an undeciphered Bronze Age inscribed object.  
> No phonetic key is provided. Based solely on your knowledge of Luwian Hieroglyphic  
> phonology and the statistics below, please assign Luwian phonetic values to the  
> structurally prominent signs. There is no "correct answer" — we are testing whether  
> independent linguistic reasoning converges on the same assignments.

```
STRUCTURAL STATISTICS — UNDECIPHERED BRONZE AGE TEXT
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Object:      Fired clay disc, double-sided spiral, ~1700 BCE, Aegean region
Signs:       45 distinct sign types, 241 total tokens, 61 word-groups
Entropy:     H = 3.045 bits  →  syllabic script range (CV syllabary likely)
Script dir.: Right-to-left, outside → center (confirmed by sign orientation Z=+7.79)

TOP SIGN FREQUENCIES:
  Sign A: 19 occurrences (7.9%)
  Sign B: 18 occurrences (7.5%)
  Sign C: 17 occurrences (7.1%)
  Sign D: 15 occurrences (6.2%)
  Sign E: 12 occurrences (5.0%)

POSITIONAL STATISTICS:
  Sign A — word-INITIAL in 19/19 occurrences (100%), Z=+7.51 vs random
  Sign F — word-FINAL in ~30% of word-final positions
  Sign G — word-FINAL in ~26% of word-final positions

SEQUENTIAL STATISTICS:
  Top bigram: Sign H → Sign F, 17 occurrences (obs/exp = 7.69×, Z=+10)
  This bigram appears in 28% of all 61 word-groups

STRUCTURAL CENTER:
  Sign I — appears at the innermost word-group of both Side A and Side B
           (the structural climax of the spiral)

TEXT TYPE:
  7 exact word-group repetitions out of 61 total (24.6% refrain density)
  Z=+45.60 vs random → almost certainly a ritual hymn or liturgical formula
```

**Question for the specialist:**  
Given that this is a ~1700 BCE syllabic text with Aegean-Anatolian contact zone context,  
and assuming the script is related to Luwian Hieroglyphic:

1. What Luwian phonetic value would you assign to **Sign A** (100% word-initial)?
2. What Luwian phonetic value would you assign to the **Sign H → Sign F bigram** (most frequent 2-sign sequence)?
3. What Luwian phonetic value would you assign to **Sign F** (word-final suffix, 30%)?
4. What Luwian phonetic value would you assign to **Sign G** (word-final suffix, 26%)?
5. What Luwian phonetic value would you assign to **Sign I** (structural center, deity invocation)?
