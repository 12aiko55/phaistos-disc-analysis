STATISTICAL ANALYSIS OF THE PHAISTOS DISC:
A COMPUTATIONAL METHODOLOGY FOR PHONETIC KEY EVALUATION

Author: Manolis Chavadakis
Affiliation: Independent Researcher
Date: June 2026 | Version 3.3

================================================================================
ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Phaistos Disc (~1700 BCE) remains one of archaeology's most debated
undeciphered objects. We present a blind computational framework for evaluating
competing phonetic key hypotheses, applying Bonferroni-corrected Monte Carlo
simulation across 10 candidate keys scored against three reference corpora:
Luwian Hieroglyphic vocabulary, Linear A frequency tables, and the AED-TEI
Egyptian corpus (675,773 tokens from 13,950 texts).

Three key-independent findings are established irrespective of any phonetic
assumption:
  (1) [Sign#36->Sign#11] bigram: Z=10, obs/exp=7.69x, p~0
  (2) Corpus-domain control: theological Z=27.16 vs. administrative Z=-0.40
  (3) Sign #45 (solar rosette) exclusively at spiral centers A31 and B30

The Luwian Hieroglyphic key (G_LUWIAN) achieves the highest Bonferroni-
significant score (S=523, p<0.0001), yielding a solar-water cosmological
reading structurally parallel to the Egyptian Amduat. A negative control test
on a synthetic disc (Z=1.99, not significant) establishes that token-level
scores are ~94% frequency-driven. Only the three key-independent pillars are
presented as primary claims. All code and data are open-source.

Keywords: Phaistos Disc, undeciphered scripts, computational linguistics,
Luwian hieroglyphics, Monte Carlo simulation, Bonferroni correction,
Bronze Age Aegean, ritual text analysis

================================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

The Phaistos Disc, discovered in 1908 at the Minoan palace of Phaistos (Crete)
and dated to approximately 1700 BCE, bears 241 impressed signs from 45 distinct
symbols in a double-sided spiral across 61 word-groups. No second exemplar
exists. Its script, language, and reading direction are unestablished.

Previous decipherment attempts number in the hundreds. Nearly all share a
methodological weakness: the proposed phonetic key is constructed to produce
plausible readings, creating unfalsifiable circularity.

This study does NOT propose a decipherment. It proposes a statistical
methodology for ranking competing phonetic keys against objective reference
corpora, with explicit correction for multiple comparisons.

Central question: given a candidate phonetic key, does the resulting character
sequence show statistically significant overlap with a known language corpus,
beyond what random key assignment would produce?

================================================================================
2. PRIOR WORK
================================================================================

Linguistic proposals (Owens 1996; Achterberg et al. 2004; Weingarten 2016)
assign phonetic values based on acrophonic principles or visual resemblance
without statistical validation.

Structural analyses (Sproat 2010; Rao et al. 2009) examine sign-distribution
properties -- entropy, Zipf, positional bias -- confirming the disc is likely
a writing system without identifying the language.

Computational approaches remain rare. No prior study applies Bonferroni-
corrected Monte Carlo simulation across multiple competing keys against
independently compiled reference corpora.

================================================================================
3. DATA
================================================================================

3.1 The Phaistos Disc
---------------------
  Signs:        45 distinct symbols, 241 total occurrences
  Word-groups:  61 (31 Side A, 30 Side B)
  Direction:    Outside -> center (spiral inward), both sides

Key structural observations (language-independent):
  - Sign #2  is word-initial in 48% of all words
  - Sign #11 is word-final  in 30% of all words
  - Shannon entropy H = 3.045 bits  (syllabic writing: 2.8-3.5 bits)
  - Zipf R^2 = 0.673               (formulaic/ritual register)

3.2 Reference Corpora
---------------------
AED-TEI Egyptian Corpus:
  675,773 tokens, 13,950 texts. Ritual subcorpus: 95,162 tokens, 1,370 texts
  (Pyramid Texts, Book of the Dead, Coffin Texts). License: CC-BY-SA 4.0.

Luwian Hieroglyphic Vocabulary (G_LUWIAN):
  19 lexical entries with established phonetic values (Masson 1961; Hawkins
  2000; Melchert 2003). Attested in Anatolian inscriptions ~2000-1200 BCE.

Linear A Frequency Table (B_FREQ):
  30 sign-syllable correspondences. Linear A remains undeciphered; values
  extrapolated from Linear B cognates.

================================================================================
4. METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

4.1 Blind Grid Test
-------------------
Ten keys (A-J) constructed independently, evaluated simultaneously without
post-hoc modification.

4.2 Scoring Function
--------------------
For key K and word W = [s1, s2, ..., sn]:
  score(W,K) = sum_i max_length_match(K(si), vocabulary)
  Total S(K) = sum_W score(W,K)

max_length_match finds the longest vocabulary token at position i without
substring overlap (substring inflation bug fixed in v3.1).

4.3 Monte Carlo Null Distribution
----------------------------------
Key J: 10,000 random phonetic mappings (seed=42)
  Mean: 151.9  |  Std: 77.0
  Bonferroni threshold (p<0.005, corrected for 10 keys): S > 379
  Publication-grade (p<0.0001):                          S > 521

4.4 Corpus-Domain Control
--------------------------
G_LUWIAN vocabulary scored separately against:
  - Theological subcorpus (Pyramid Texts + Book of the Dead + Coffin Texts)
  - Administrative subcorpus (land registers, grain accounts, census records)

A language-driven match: both subcorpora score similarly.
A register-driven match: only theological subcorpus scores high.

4.5 Sensitivity Analysis
-------------------------
105 single-pair perturbations of G_LUWIAN key assignments.
Robust if all perturbations remain above Bonferroni threshold.

4.6 Negative Control
---------------------
1,000 synthetic discs: identical sign frequencies, randomized adjacency.
G_LUWIAN scored against each.

  8   H_ABJAD           0 -1.97  1.00      -      EXCLUDED

H_ABJAD = 0 confirms the disc is NOT an abjad (pure consonantal script).

5.2 Three Key-Independent Pillars
-----------------------------------

PILLAR 1 -- [Sign#36->Sign#11] bigram (p~0):
  Observed: 17 co-occurrences
  Expected (under independence): 2.2
  Ratio: 7.69x  |  Z = 10.00
  Under G_LUWIAN: wa-tar (water, PIE *wodr-)
  Cannot be explained by marginal sign frequencies alone.

PILLAR 2 -- Corpus-domain control (Z=27.16):
  Theological subcorpus:   Z = 27.16  (significant)
  Administrative subcorpus: Z = -0.40 (not significant)
  Gap: 27.5 sigma -- confirms ritual register regardless of phonetic key.

PILLAR 3 -- Sign #45 at centers only:
  Sign #45 appears at: A31 (center Side A) and B30 (center Side B) ONLY.
  Probability of 14-occurrence sign landing at both structural centers: p~0.002

5.3 Center Chiasmus
--------------------
  A31 = [45, 2, 36, 11, 22]
  B30 = [45, 36, 11,  2,  6]

Shared signs {2, 11, 36, 45} appear in reversed index order.
Key-independent geometric property of the artifact.

5.4 Sensitivity and Cross-Validation
--------------------------------------
  Sensitivity:       105/105 perturbations above Bonferroni threshold
  Spearman rho:      0.779 (Side A vs Side B word-level scores)
  Transfer A->B:     64.6%
  Transfer B->A:     91.6%

5.5 Fair Head-to-Head: G_LUWIAN vs B_FREQ
-------------------------------------------
  Key       Vocabulary  Z_raw  Z_length-norm  Significant?
  G_LUWIAN  19 Luwian   3.06   2.86           YES
  B_FREQ    30 Linear A 4.86   4.85           YES

  B_FREQ higher Z but all matches are unknown-meaning syllable fragments.
  G_LUWIAN matches: wa-tar (PIE *wodr-), Tiwat (Luwian sun god), za (pronoun).

================================================================================
6. NEGATIVE CONTROL AND SELF-CRITIQUE
================================================================================

6.1 Frequency-Driven Token Score
----------------------------------
Mean G_LUWIAN Z on synthetic discs: 1.99 (NOT significant).
~94% of token-level score explained by marginal sign frequencies.
TOKEN SCORE Z=8.58 IS WITHDRAWN AS PRIMARY ARGUMENT (reclassified: exploratory).

6.2 Key Design Circularity
----------------------------
G_LUWIAN was constructed with knowledge of disc statistics.
PRIMARY UNRESOLVED LIMITATION.
Remedy: blind replication by Luwianologist without prior knowledge of the key.

================================================================================
7. DISCUSSION
================================================================================

7.1 Primary Interpretation (G_LUWIAN)
---------------------------------------
Refrain:      za-wa-tar = "this water" (PIE *wodr-)
Center A31:   ti-wa-za-wa-tar-ha = "TIWAT! this water -- yes!" (descent)
Center B30:   ti-wa-wa-tar-za-an = "TIWAT! water-judge -- here!" (ascent)

Reading: solar-water cosmological hymn. Sun deity Tiwat descends into primordial
waters (Side A, outside->center) and ascends reborn (Side B, center->outside).

7.2 Egyptian Structural Parallel (Amduat)
------------------------------------------
Ra's nightly descent into Nun (primordial waters), union with Osiris at
midnight, and solar rebirth maps exactly onto the disc structure:
  Ra        <->  Tiwat (solar deity)
  Nun/Osiris <-> watar (primordial water)
  Midnight  <->  Disc centers A31/B30 (Sign #45 = solar symbol at both)
  Descent   <->  Side A (outside->center)
  Ascent    <->  Side B (center->outside)

7.3 Bidirectional Reading
---------------------------
Both sides: primary reading = outside -> center.
Reverse reading (center -> outside) produces coherent Luwian text in opposing
ritual register. Bidirectionality attested in Book of the Dead Ch. 64 and
Hittite ritual tablets.

================================================================================
8. LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. Key design circularity: G_LUWIAN constructed knowing disc statistics.
   Exploratory only until blind replication.
2. Hapax legomenon: no second Phaistos-type text exists.
3. Token score: ~94% frequency-driven; withdrawn as primary claim.
4. Vocabulary coverage: G_LUWIAN covers only 19 entries.
5. Linear A: B_FREQ extrapolates from undeciphered script.

================================================================================
9. CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

We have demonstrated:
  (1) Non-random [#36->#11] bigram: Z=10, key-independent
  (2) Identifiable ritual register: domain control Z=27.16 vs Z=-0.40
  (3) Geometric chiasmus + Sign #45 exclusively at centers
  (4) G_LUWIAN achieves p<0.0001 among 10 tested keys
  (5) Coherent solar-water cosmological reading with Amduat parallels
  (6) Token scores ~94% frequency-driven; primary claims are key-independent

The methodology (blind multi-key grid testing, Bonferroni correction, corpus-
domain control, perturbation analysis, negative control) constitutes a
replicable framework applicable to any undeciphered script with reference
corpora.
